# **Security Auditing Process (Made Easy)**

Think of a **security audit** like a **health check-up for an organization’s IT systems**.  
Doctors = Auditors, Patient = Company, Health = Security.

---

### **1. Planning & Preparation**

- **What happens?** Decide **what to check**, collect past reports, and build an audit team.
    
- **Example:** A hospital wants an audit of its patient database system. The audit team collects the hospital’s IT policies and past issues.
    

---

### **2. Information Gathering**

- **What happens?** Collect details about systems, policies, and practices. Interview staff.
    
- **Example:** The auditors talk to IT staff, review password policies, and check how the hospital’s network is set up.
    

---

### **3. Risk Assessment**

- **What happens?** Identify **what’s valuable (assets)**, **what could go wrong (threats)**, and **how weak the defenses are (vulnerabilities)**.
    
- **Example:** The hospital’s patient database = asset. Threat = hackers. Vulnerability = weak passwords. Risk = high.
    

---

### **4. Audit Execution**

- **What happens?** Perform **technical testing** (scans, penetration tests) and check compliance with laws/standards.
    
- **Example:** Auditors run a vulnerability scan → find that the hospital’s database server has an old, unpatched bug.
    

---

### **5. Analysis & Evaluation**

- **What happens?** Review findings, compare with standards, and prioritize issues.
    
- **Example:** They find three issues: weak passwords, unpatched software, missing encryption. They rank encryption as **critical**.
    

---

### **6. Reporting**

- **What happens?** Write a **report** with findings + recommendations. Present to management.
    
- **Example:** The audit report says:
    
    - Weak passwords → use MFA
        
    - Old server → patch/update
        
    - Missing encryption → enable data encryption
        

---

### **7. Remediation (Fixing Problems)**

- **What happens?** The company fixes issues, auditors check again later.
    
- **Example:** The hospital enforces MFA, updates servers, and encrypts patient records. Auditors return later to confirm the fixes.
    

---

## 🔄 **Security Auditing Lifecycle (Easy View)**

1. **Plan** → What to check
    
2. **Gather Info** → Collect details
    
3. **Risk Assessment** → Find risks
    
4. **Execution** → Test systems
    
5. **Analysis** → Review results
    
6. **Report** → Share findings
    
7. **Remediation** → Fix problems
